Hands-On!

Rather than theory!

This is the site plan for my WDD 131 final project: a website about Apple iOS devices, told from real daily use by me.

Site name

Hands-On!

The name means that the site talks about Apple iOS devices from real use, not from specification sheets. I own these devices and use them every day, so I can show the quality of the products and how the iOS system really works, with my own photos. The lens in the logo represents those photos, and that is why the tagline is "Rather than theory!"

Site purpose

  • Explain each Apple iOS product from real use, in simple words: what it does and who it is for.
  • Show original pictures of the devices, taken by me, as real examples.
  • Let visitors ask questions or suggest a product through a contact form.

Planned pages: Home, Products, Contact, and a References page.

Scenarios

  • What are the main differences between the current iPhone models, and which one should I choose?
  • What can an iPad do that my iPhone cannot, and is it worth it for studying?
  • How do the photos look when they are taken with a real iPhone camera in everyday light?

Color scheme

These colors are used on this document and will be used on the final site.

  • Midnight #0B1F3A Page background, headings, and body text on white cards.
  • Paper #F3F6FA Text on dark backgrounds, such as the hero description.
  • Lens Teal #006B76 Footer background, section titles, links, and buttons.
  • Amber #F2A900 Header bar and small accents such as highlights.
  • White #FFFFFF Content card backgrounds.
