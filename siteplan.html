About

Hi there! This is Kevin.

I have been an Apple user since 2014, when there was a serious import problem in Argentina. Thanks to my dad, I was able to get my first iPhone: an iPhone 6! From there I learned that a good product must be recognized not just because of marketing, but because of real experience.

Reason for this website

Feeling comfortable when you take a picture, when you work, or when you study a hard course matters. An Apple device can help you feel comfortable and productive, because its software has many tools to improve your time. That is why I now work for a remote company using my MacBook Air, I take pictures with my iPhone 17 Pro, and I study on a 13-inch iPad Air. Why did I choose them?
